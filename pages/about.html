Our House

The art of choosing well.

Founded in Kate’s name, built around discernment.

The Kate Treasury is a digital house for fine jewellery and decorative antiques. Its identity draws on the intimacy of a private collection: fewer distractions, considered presentation and objects that reward a closer look.

Our purpose is to present beauty with clarity. Jewellery is approached as wearable architecture; antiques as living history. Every future item should be documented honestly, photographed carefully and described with appropriate condition, measurements, materials and provenance.

Our principles

Beauty with substance. Form matters, but craftsmanship, condition and story matter too.

Discretion by design. High-value enquiries should feel personal rather than transactional.

Responsible representation. Verified details replace exaggeration, and provenance claims appear only when supported.
